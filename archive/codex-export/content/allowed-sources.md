This project uses the 2014 rules version of 5e.tools as its source index. Character options must be present at [2014.5e.tools](https://2014.5e.tools/) unless they are covered by an explicitly recorded campaign exception.

## Source policy

The campaign rules baseline remains **2014 D&D 5e plus Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything**, with the homebrew and DM rulings recorded in `rules/homebrew-and-rulings.md`.

## Allowed source categories

- Official 2014 core material available on the site, including the Player’s Handbook, Dungeon Master’s Guide, and Monster Manual.

- Official 2014-era supplements available on the site, including Xanathar’s Guide to Everything, Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything, Volo’s Guide to Monsters, Mordenkainen’s Tome of Foes, and other 2014-compatible official books.

- Official adventure and setting books only where the specific option is present on the 2014 site and is relevant to the character.

- Options from any class spell list when legally copied as a ritual into Book of Ancient Secrets, provided the spell is marked as a ritual and appears in an allowed 2014 source.

## Explicit exclusions

- 2024-only rules and revised 2024 books.

- Content that appears only on the non-2014 5e.tools site.

- Unearthed Arcana, third-party material, and homebrew unless separately approved by the DM.

- The 2024 Sphinx of Wonder / “Spirit of Wonder” familiar concept is not currently part of the allowed rules baseline.

- Robe of Thayan Arcana is not approved and is excluded from the build.

## Project-specific exceptions

The campaign has explicitly approved or assumed the following homebrew elements:

- Additional level-1 feat, used for Telekinetic.

- Half-Elf changes recorded in `rules/homebrew-and-rulings.md`.

- Patron expanded spells count as additional spells known.

- Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast use the campaign’s once-per-target wording.

- Ten attunement slots.

These are campaign rules, not claims that the options are printed in an allowed source.

## Checking rule

When adding a new option to the project, record its 5e.tools source abbreviation and mark it as one of:

- **Current** — legal under the source policy and part of the build.

- **Option** — legal under the source policy but not selected.

- **Needs DM approval** — outside the source policy or dependent on a campaign ruling.
