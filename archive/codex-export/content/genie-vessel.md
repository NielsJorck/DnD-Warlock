The Genie Vessel is more than an inventory container: it is Erdan’s fully stocked extradimensional home, workshop, and refuge. Its interior functions broadly like Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion, with its furnishings, comforts, and practical supplies shaped by the Vessel’s earth, stone, crystal, and mineral identity.

## Core Vessel functions

- **Fully stocked interior:** The Vessel provides the rooms, furnishings, food, drink, domestic conveniences, and workshop equipment expected of a luxurious magical residence.

- **Noncombat magic and flavor:** The Vessel may contain any magic item that is noncombat-focused, domestic, practical, restorative, artistic, scholarly, or primarily useful for flavor. These items are part of the Vessel’s setting and identity rather than a substitute for combat equipment.

- **Bag of Holding:** The Vessel can also be used as a Bag of Holding for storing and retrieving objects. Items stored this way remain part of the Vessel’s contents unless explicitly moved out.

## Catnip-like rest feature

Once per long rest, the Vessel can provide the equivalent of a short rest in 10 minutes. This accelerated rest is a special property of the Vessel and does not grant additional uses until the next long rest.

## Magnificent Mansion model

The Vessel uses Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion as its design model:

- It opens into a clean, warm, comfortable extradimensional residence with a foyer and multiple chambers.

- Its layout can include living quarters, dining areas, libraries, workshops, storage, bathing facilities, and other rooms that fit the Vessel’s identity.

- The residence is lavishly furnished and can provide food, drink, domestic supplies, and other noncombat comforts for the people inside.

- Its magical attendants may clean, cook, mend, fetch, organize, light fires, serve guests, and perform other harmless household tasks, but they cannot attack or directly harm creatures.

- The Vessel’s furnishings and ordinary created provisions are meant for use inside the Vessel. Any unusual object or magic item that is removed from it should be treated as part of the Vessel’s inventory and governed by the DM’s ruling on that item.

The spell’s normal temporary duration is not the point of the adaptation; the Vessel is a persistent item-based home that uses the spell’s extradimensional mansion as its flavor and functional template.

## Features

- **Extradimensional residence:** A persistent, comfortable home with a foyer and multiple customizable rooms.

- **Fully stocked household:** Food, drink, furnishings, lighting, bathing facilities, domestic supplies, and workshop materials are available within the Vessel.

- **Harmless magical attendants:** Servants can cook, clean, mend, fetch, organize, light fires, serve guests, and perform other ordinary household tasks, but cannot attack or directly harm creatures.

- **Noncombat magic-item integration:** Noncombat, domestic, scholarly, restorative, artistic, or flavor-focused magic items may be incorporated into the Vessel’s rooms and systems.

- **Bag of Holding function:** The Vessel can store and retrieve objects as an extradimensional container.

- **Accelerated short rest:** Once per long rest, the Vessel can provide the equivalent of a short rest in 10 minutes.

- **Room adaptation:** The layout and furnishings can be changed to suit Erdan’s current needs, provided the changes remain within the Vessel’s noncombat and flavor-focused purpose.

## Vessel role

The Vessel is a practical home, refuge, and workshop with an earth, stone, crystal, and mineral identity. It is fully stocked for comfortable living and creative, scholarly, domestic, and restorative pursuits. Its inventory emphasizes preservation, food, water, lighting, cleaning, language, measurement, repair, and other noncombat utility.

## Separation rule

The Vessel itself includes the Bag of Holding function described above, so a separate Bag of Holding is not required for the concept. Any future extradimensional-space item still requires explicit consideration of how it interacts with the Genie Vessel.
