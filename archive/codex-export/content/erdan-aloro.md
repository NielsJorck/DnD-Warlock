Erdan Aloro is a Half-Elf whose professional life was built around gemstones, precious metals, and unusual minerals. Trained through a guild as a jeweler, gemcutter, and appraiser, he learned to judge craftsmanship, value, provenance, authenticity, and the people on the other side of a negotiation. In his trade, reputation mattered, and so did the exact wording of an agreement.

His work eventually brought him into contact with something outside ordinary commerce: a rare stone, crafted object, or commission tied to Zahir, a Noble Dao of the Elemental Plane of Earth. The exact details of the first encounter remain campaign-flexible. The important point is that Erdan did not become a servant or worshipper. He entered into an agreement.

That pact shaped his magic and outlook. Erdan favors control, positioning, careful preparation, and dependable tools over reckless destruction. His command of Terran reflects his connection to the Plane of Earth. His Vessel has become a private home of stone, crystal, mineral, and worked metal.

## Personality themes

- Exacting without being needlessly cruel.

- Values craftsmanship, provenance, and promises.

- Sees magical items as tools with histories and obligations, not disposable upgrades.

- Finds creative uses for mundane objects and controlled battlefield movement.

- Is comfortable negotiating with dangerous beings when the terms are clear.
