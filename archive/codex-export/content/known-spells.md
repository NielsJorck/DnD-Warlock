## Book of Shadows

| Level | Ritual | Concentration | Source | Primary role |
| --- | --- | :---: | --- | --- |
| 1st | [Detect Magic](/detect-magic) | ✅ | Book of Shadows | Detect and identify nearby magic |
| 1st | [Identify](/identify) | ❌ | Book of Shadows | Learn an item’s magical properties |

Book of Ancient Secrets allows qualifying ritual spells to be copied from written sources. At level 10, the working cap is rituals up to 5th level. The full aspirational list and priorities are on the [Book of Shadows — Ritual Wishlist](/book-of-shadows-ritual-wishlist) subpage. Copying costs 50 gp and 2 hours per spell level, and the spell must be marked as a ritual; merely knowing a spell is not enough.
