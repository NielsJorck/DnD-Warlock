# Spell Reference

This page is the spell glossary. Each entry below links to its own spell subpage, where the full 2014 rules description is kept. Other character and planning pages should link to those individual spell pages.

## Rules and reference policy

Patron expanded spells are treated as additional spells known, rather than merely being added to the Warlock spell list. Therefore, the ten 1st–5th-level Genie/Dao spells below do not consume Erdan’s normal Warlock spells-known allowance. Access source: Genie Patron / Dao. Publication source: normally *Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything*. Wish is retained as a future patron spell, but is unavailable to a level-10 Warlock.
