## Character identity

Erdan is a jeweler, gemcutter, and appraiser whose pact is an agreement with a powerful Dao rather than an act of worship. He values preparation, leverage, exact wording, and durable solutions. Zahir’s principle is: **a deal made is a deal kept.** See `story/erdan.md` and `story/zahir.md`.
