## Campaign homebrew

- Every character gains an additional feat at level 1; Erdan uses it for Telekinetic.

- Half-Elf Darkvision is reduced to 30 ft.

- Half-Elf Skill Versatility grants Investigation.

- Pact of the Tome treats the patron expanded spell list as additional spells known rather than merely additional spell options.

- Agonizing Blast adds Charisma damage once per target, not once per beam.

- Repelling Blast pushes once per target, not once per beam.

- The campaign attunement limit is 10 items.

## Current DM rulings and assumptions

- Robe of Thayan Arcana is not approved and is not included in statistics.

- Bag of Holding remains outside the Genie Vessel until extradimensional-space interaction is ruled safe.

- The dagger-shaped Rod of the Pact Keeper is cosmetic and mechanically a rod unless separately approved as a weapon.

## Items still requiring explicit confirmation

- Whether the additional Guild Artisan language can be selected as another useful language; Terran is already the Half-Elf language choice.

- Whether any future item can be treated as both a rod and a weapon without changing its magic-item rules.

- Exact interaction between the Genie Vessel and other extradimensional spaces.
