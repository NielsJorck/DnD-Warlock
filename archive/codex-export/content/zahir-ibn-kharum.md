Zahir is a powerful Noble Dao whose interests center on stone, crystal, gemstones, precious metals, craftsmanship, wealth, and permanence. He values things that endure: structures, treasures, reputations, and obligations.

## Relationship with Erdan

Zahir does not view Erdan as a worshipper. Their relationship is based on negotiation, mutual obligation, and trust in the agreement itself. Zahir may be demanding, calculating, and precise, but he applies the same standard to himself as he applies to Erdan.

The pact can contain tension whenever the exact wording of a deal matters. It nevertheless has a stable foundation: Zahir considers his own word binding.

**Core principle:** A deal made is a deal kept.

## Campaign hooks

- Zahir asks Erdan to locate, appraise, recover, or authenticate rare minerals and magical artifacts.

- Zahir tests whether Erdan honors the spirit as well as the letter of agreements.

- A seemingly valuable object may be important because of provenance, ownership, or an old obligation.

- The Genie Vessel can gradually reveal Zahir’s taste for permanence, luxury, and controlled spaces.
